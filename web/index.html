How coverage is worked out
  • Detect & model (X) can learn from samples: box one wall, one door, one window, one object or one hydrant symbol on the plan, and every match on the sheet is modelled in 2D and 3D. Wall samples teach the wall thickness, fill style and ink tone. Interior partitions are also looked for as lighter, thinner double lines, so they are found even when the outer walls are drawn solid. Door, window, object and hydrant samples are searched for as symbols in all eight orientations. Doors and windows are cut into the nearest wall, joining wall pieces across the opening. Without samples, detection reads the plan's thick lines as walls, horizontal and vertical ones. Collinear wall ends with a gap between the door sizes you set become doors; a gap crossed by thin glazing lines becomes a window and stays solid. Detected walls keep the thickness measured on the plan. Detection runs by itself when you import a plan into an empty sketch. The Pick tool (L) turns one line at a time into a wall: hover to highlight it, click to add it. With "Every line is a wall" ticked, every stroked line in a PDF (any angle) longer than the minimum wall length becomes a wall of the Criteria wall thickness; lines in line with a door-sized gap between them get a door in the gap; line ends that stop just short of another line are joined to it. For images the thin horizontal and vertical lines are traced instead.
  • The hose is routed from each hydrant along the shortest path around walls and hose-blocking objects, through door openings only. Anywhere within the hose length is a possible nozzle position. At every doorway the hose lines up on the door's centre line in front of it, goes straight through the middle of the opening and comes out square on the other side, never cutting across the door jamb or leaf. Internal hydrants (red) use the internal hose length, 30 m by default; external hydrants (blue) use the external hose length, 60 m by default. Both then add the 10 m stream. Internal FH, external FH and hose reel coverage can each be shown or hidden with the three Coverage buttons (blue, purple, green); nothing is hatched until you switch on one of the three Hatch gaps buttons in the toolbar (red: internal FH, purple: external FH, green: hose reel), which hatch the floor that equipment cannot reach in its own colour. Equipment placed or dragged near a wall mounts on it, turned to sit against the wall face (hold Alt to place freely), and can be rotated afterwards. Fire hose reels (green) are a separate system: 36 m of hose plus a 4 m stream by default, 40 m in total, with their coverage shown in green. The Coverage switch shows hydrants, hose reels, or both; in Both the floor is hatched wherever a system in use does not reach. When checking a hose run, the full hose length is laid out first along the route and the stream only covers what is left, so a point 38 m along the route from an internal hydrant shows 30 m of hose and an 8 m stream.
  • An external hydrant's hose is laid around the outside of the building to a point 1 m in front of an external door, on the door's centre line, and is then taken straight in through the door. It never cuts in diagonally from the door jamb. External doors are the doors with open ground on one side and the building on the other.
  • The stream does not pass through doorways. To protect a room the hose has to be taken through the door and at least the "nozzle inside room" distance (1 m) past it, and the 10 m stream works from there.
  • From every nozzle position the stream travels inside the same room in a straight line up to the stream length, stopped by walls and full-height objects (cupboards, shelving, columns, plant). Tables and desks block the hose but not the stream.
  • Floor inside the protected area that no stream reaches is hatched. With no Area drawn, the enclosed rooms are found from your walls (doors treated as closed).
  • 3D shows the same model; the cube in the top-right corner turns with it, and clicking a face (Top, Front, Back, Left, Right) or ⌂ jumps to that view. Name tags are off by default (tick Labels to show small ones): extruded walls with door and window openings, open door leaves, objects at their real heights, the hose laid on the floor, the stream arcing to each hose-run point, and the coverage painted on the floor.
  • Keys: 1, 2, 3 switch 2D / 3D / Split. Shift + mouse wheel zooms the sketch, the wheel alone pans. Enter or double-click finishes a wall. Esc cancels. Delete removes the selection.
